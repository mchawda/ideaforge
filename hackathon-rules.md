## Overview

36-hour in-person hackathon at **Marina Bay Sands, Singapore**. 200 builders compete for **$200K+** in prizes, credits, and resources. Top 5 teams demo live on the **WEKA stage** at SuperAI in front of 10,000 attendees.

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Venue | Marina Bay Sands, 10 Bayfront Avenue, Singapore 018956 |
| Duration | 36 hours |
| Team size | 1–4 members |
| Attendance | In-person only — full 36 hours required |
| Prize pool | $125K AWS Credits + track sponsor prizes |

---

## Theme

**Build anything — but make it agentic.**

The problem domain is open (productivity, finance, healthcare, creative tools, infrastructure, etc.). What matters is **how your agents solve it** — agents must do real work at the core of the project.

---

## Fair Play Rules

- **No pre-work:** All project work must begin after the official hacking period starts (12:00pm, 9 June). Brainstorming ideas is allowed; no code, designs, or prototypes before kickoff.
- **In-person only:** Physical attendance required for the full 36 hours. No remote participation.
- **One submission per team.**

## Submission Requirements

**Deadline:** 11:59pm, 10 June (12:00am, 11 June). **No late entries.**

Every team must submit:

1. **GitHub repo** — public, or with judge access granted
2. **Project link** — live URL or hosted demo
3. **Presentation slides** — Google Drive link to a `.ppt` or `.keynote` file

### Slide rules (for Top 5 finalists pitching on WEKA stage)

- Format must be **`.ppt` or `.keynote`** — Google Slides, Gamma, and Vercel page links are not accepted
- Use **embedded screen recordings** to showcase demos — live demos on stage are prohibited
- Do not link to YouTube or external video
- Keep slides visual and punchy — they are a visual aid for your live pitch
- **Slides are locked at submission** — no changes after the deadline

## Judging Criteria

| Dimension | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| **Agent Overview** | What agent(s) did you build? What is their purpose? |
| **Autonomy & Decision-Making** | How does your agent decide what to do next? Reasoning, planning, tool-use patterns |
| **Actions & Tool Use** | What actions can your agent take? What tools/APIs/environments does it interact with? |
| **Orchestration** | If multi-agent: how do agents coordinate, delegate, or communicate? |
| **Human-in-the-Loop** | Where does a human intervene, approve, or override? |
| **Failure Handling** | How does your agent recover from errors or unexpected states? |
| **Demo & Presentation** | Clarity of pitch, quality of demo/product, communication |

### How to submit

1. **Log in** to DoraHacks (GitHub login recommended — you're registered as `mchawda`).
2. Open the [hackathon detail page](https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/next-hackathon/detail).
3. Click **Submit BUIDL**.
4. Choose **Create new BUIDL** (or **Use existing BUIDL** if you already created one).
5. Fill in:
   - Project name & description
   - **GitHub repo** (required — public or grant judge access)
   - **Demo / project link** (live URL)
   - **Presentation slides** (Google Drive link to `.ppt` or `.keynote`)
   - Team members
6. Click **Submit for review**.
7. After submitting, the button becomes **Manage Submission** — use this to edit until the deadline.

> You can submit early and keep editing until **11:59pm, 10 June**. Submissions open **12:00pm, 9 June**.

## API Keys & Credentials

**Do not put real keys in this file.** Store them in `.env` (gitignored).

```bash
cp .env.example .env   # then fill in values
```
